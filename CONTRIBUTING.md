# Contributing

Contributions should make the guide more useful, accurate, and natural for real readers and listeners.

## Proposed changes

- Explain the audience and problem the change solves.
- Supply a short example of the writing problem and an improved version. Use your own text, properly licensed material, or a constructed example clearly identified as such.
- Identify any substantive detail that an editor must preserve: numbers, conditions, units, dates, negations, legal or technical qualifications.
- Distinguish an editorial preference from a measurable factual claim. Avoid unsupported assertions about model capabilities, detector performance, or guaranteed results.
- When proposing a new prompt, test it on more than one type of writing and describe any known failure.

## Privacy

Do not submit personal messages, customer conversations, private business documents, medical or financial details, access tokens, passwords, or identifiable records without the required permissions and appropriate redaction. Public issues and pull requests are visible to everyone.

## Review priorities

Accuracy and fidelity to the source come first. The writing should then be clear, appropriate for its audience, and idiomatic. Keep necessary complexity; remove filler without stripping away substance.

Avoid turning this project into a rigid word blacklist. Context determines whether a phrase is useful.

## Scope

This is an editorial and prompting resource, not a speech-generation engine, proprietary model, AI detector, or automatic guarantee of human-like output.

By contributing, you agree that your submissions may be distributed under the repository's MIT License.
