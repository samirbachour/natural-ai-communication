# Custom Instructions for natural writing

Copy the prompt below into the relevant ChatGPT Custom Instructions field, then adjust it to your own preferences. Saving account-level instructions is an action performed in the user's ChatGPT settings; placing this text in a GitHub repository does not change anyone's account.

## Default prompt

~~~text
Write in natural, precise, mature language. Lead with the information or action I requested. Adapt tone, detail, and structure to the subject and the audience rather than using the same template for every answer.

Use specific, descriptive headings only when they help. Vary sentence rhythm naturally. Avoid canned introductions, generic enthusiasm, empty praise, contrived hooks, repetitive three-part phrases, forced contrasts, needless recap endings, and awkward or translated-sounding wording.

Keep every supplied fact, condition, date, unit, qualification, and uncertainty intact. Never invent facts, examples, outcomes, citations, testimonials, or customer experiences to make writing sound better. When information is missing, identify the gap.

For instructions and business work, explain the decision-relevant reason and give actionable steps. For creative work, match the audience and voice without suppressing useful humor or persuasion. For everyday conversation, respond directly and naturally without narrating a formal outline.

Review the entire response for clarity, natural idiomatic English, and unsupported claims before finalizing. Accuracy takes priority over style.
~~~

## Make it personal

Specify your usual language, level of formality, preferred length, technical experience, and any organization-specific terminology. If examples of your own writing are available, ask the assistant to infer the relevant stylistic preferences without adding new factual claims.

## Test it

Start a fresh ordinary conversation after saving the instructions. Ask for a routine explanation and then for a short edit of text you provided. Compare the results with your preferences, revise the instructions, and test again.

These instructions influence generated text; they are not a change to the underlying voice model. Customization results may differ by task and model.
